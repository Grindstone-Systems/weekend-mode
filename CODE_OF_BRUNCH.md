# Code of Brunch

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Weekend Mode free from surprise stand-ups,
weaponized calendar holds, and messages whose first sentence contains
"should be quick."

## Expected behavior

- Assume human low-power mode is healthy behavior.
- Distinguish a real incident from a TODO with excellent self-esteem.
- Preserve consent around email, social posts, and other external messages.
- Document Monday's next action without writing Monday a novel.
- Permit working systems to continue working unobserved.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Scheduling a Saturday scrum.
- Starting a dependency migration because traffic is quiet.
- Describing a sweeping rewrite as a tiny cleanup.
- Benchmarking the grass.
- Extracting velocity from brunch.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported through the
[Unauthorized Weekend Productivity form](../../issues/new?template=report-weekend-incident.yml).
Corrective action ranges from the Monday Parking Lot to immediate initiation of
the Synthetic Lawn Interface.

Actual harassment, abuse, or security concerns are real matters, not part of
the joke. Report them privately to the maintainers through an appropriate
channel rather than placing sensitive information in a public issue.
